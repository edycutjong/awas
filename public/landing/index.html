tanya jawab

Questions a judge would ask.

Does Awas ever say an offer is safe?

No. NO RED FLAGS FOUND says on screen that it is not a guarantee. No score, no green.

Who decides STOP — the model?

Code: verdict.ts. A sourced NEVER rule, or a verified line naming the offer’s own company beside a harm word. In tranche 2, 8 of 9 STOPs came from rules.

What if the model invents a quote?

The gate drops it, on screen — 6 of 54 in tranche 2. Flip one word in the lab and watch.

How accurate is it?

Out of sample: 0/10 licensed vacancies stopped; 25 reported scam offers: 9 STOP, 15 VERIFY FIRST, 1 missed — descriptions, not ads. Every row: DEMO.md.

Does it accuse companies?

Its own sentences never name one. A name appears only in the pasted offer, a verified quote, or beside “Dilaporkan oleh <sumber>, bukan tuduhan kami” — reported by the source, not our accusation.
