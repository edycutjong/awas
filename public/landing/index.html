tanya jawab

Questions a judge would ask.

Who decides STOP — the model?

Code: verdict.ts — a sourced NEVER rule, or a verified line naming the offer’s own company beside a harm word. 8 of 9 tranche-2 STOPs came from rules. No score, no green; the mildest verdict says it is not a guarantee.

What if the model invents a quote?

The gate drops it, on screen — 6 of 54 in tranche 2. Flip one word in the lab and watch.

How accurate is it?

Out of sample: 0 of 10 licensed vacancies got STOP; of 25 reported scam offers, 9 STOP, 15 VERIFY FIRST, 1 missed — reporters’ descriptions, not the ads. Every row: bench/results-t2.jsonl.

Does it accuse companies?

Its own sentences are checked in code not to name the offer’s company. A name appears only in the pasted offer, a verified quote and its translation, or beside “Dilaporkan oleh <sumber>, bukan tuduhan kami” — reported by the source, not our accusation.

What happens to what I paste?

The text goes to Nemotron on Token Factory; only a company name or number goes to Tavily. Each check is stored in Nebius Object Storage so its link keeps working; the app says disimpan 75 hari, tidak diindeks mesin pencari, nomor telepon disamarkan — kept 75 days, not indexed, phone numbers masked (the expiry rule’s receipt is still pending). Don’t paste personal data.
